Progressive Overload Guide

      
Slightly better than last week. Every week.

    

    

      Key
      ▼
    

  

  

    

      
Add reps
1–2 more reps, same weight. 12kg×10 → 12kg×12

      
Add weight
+1–2.5 kg when you hit top of rep range. 12kg×12 → 14kg×10

      
Slow tempo
3s down · 1s pause · 2s up. More time under tension = more growth.

      
Better form
Less momentum. Harder squeeze. Drop the ego. Quality beats weight every time.

    Supplements support the plan — they don't replace food. Only whey + creatine are truly worth it for most people. Everything else is optional insurance for a vegetarian cut.
  


  
Core — take these

  

    
Whey protein — 2 scoops/day
~50g protein. 1 scoop in the 3:30–4 PM snack, 1 post-workout or with dinner. The single most important supplement for a vegetarian hitting 148g.

    
Creatine monohydrate — 5g/day
Any time, daily, even rest days. Mix in water or your shake. Most researched supplement for strength + muscle. Vegetarians respond especially well (low dietary creatine).

  


  
Recommended for vegetarians

  

    
Vitamin B12 — 1 tablet/day (or 1500mcg 2–3×/wk)
Vegetarians are commonly deficient. Affects energy + nerves. Cheap, essential.

    
Vitamin D3 — 1000–2000 IU/day
Most Indians are deficient. Take with a fatty meal (lunch). Supports testosterone, bones, mood.

    
Omega-3 (algal or flax) — daily
Veg diets are low in EPA/DHA. Algal oil capsule, or 1 tbsp ground flaxseed in your shake.

  


  
Optional

  

    
Multivitamin — 1/day with breakfast
Covers small gaps on a calorie deficit. Optional if diet is varied.

    
Black coffee — pre-workout
Free pre-workout. Boosts fasted-training performance + fat oxidation. You already drink it.

  


  

    Not needed: fat burners, BCAAs (whey already covers them), mass gainers (you're cutting), test boosters. Save your money. Whey + creatine + B12 + D3 is a complete, evidence-based stack.

Computer Networks: OSI 7 layers, TCP/IP, IP addressing, Subnetting, MAC, ARP, DNS, DHCP, Ports, Protocols

Number Systems: Binary, Octal, Hex conversions, BCD, ASCII, Logic Gates, Nibble/Byte

Software: DLL, SaaS/IaaS/PaaS, IANA, Open Source, Virtualization basics

Negative marking: DHC uses −0.25 negative marking. Skip if you're less than 60% confident. Don't guess randomly.

  
C code trace questions: These appear heavily. Practice predicting output without running code. Focus on pointers, post/pre-increment, and logical operators.

  
DBMS normalization: Expect 3–5 questions on normal forms (1NF–BCNF). "Initial Normal Form" does NOT exist — always pick "First Normal Form" as the lowest level.

  
Port numbers to memorize: HTTP=80, HTTPS=443, FTP=21, SSH=22, Telnet=23, DNS=53, SMTP=25, POP3=110. These appear every year.

  
Number conversions: Practice decimal↔binary↔octal↔hex daily in Phase 1. 5 minutes/day builds speed. Use GeeksForGeeks practice MCQs.

  
Time strategy in exam: Technical (CS) questions first — they carry more marks. Then aptitude. Leave ambiguous GK for the end. Target: 45 min tech · 30 min aptitude · 15 min GK · 10 min review.

  
Current affairs shortcut: From 3 weeks before exam, read PIB.gov.in daily for 10 min. Focus on ISRO launches, govt schemes, awards — court exam favourites.

  
MS Office questions: Know MS Word shortcuts (Format Painter = Alt+Ctrl+C), PowerPoint (Ctrl+M = new slide), Excel basics. These give easy marks.
